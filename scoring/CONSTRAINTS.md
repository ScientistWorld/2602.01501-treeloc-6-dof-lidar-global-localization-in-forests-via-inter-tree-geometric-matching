# Scientist Constraints

## Data
Use the fixed benchmark generated by `data/generate_synthetic_forest.py` with seed 11 unless explicitly reporting a separate non-comparable experiment. The query/database split, ground-truth poses, positive-distance threshold, and pose thresholds must remain unchanged for `synthetic_forest_localization`.

## Evaluation
Use `scripts/evaluate.sh` and the output schema consumed by `eval/evaluate_treeloc.py`. Do not change the 10 m positive-pair threshold, the 0.5 m translation threshold, or the 5 degree rotation threshold when submitting comparable scores.

## Inputs
Methods may use only the per-scene tree inventory fields in `data/forest_scenes.json`: local tree positions, axes, DBH, base heights, and scene names. Ground-truth global poses are reserved for evaluation and must not be used to rank candidates or estimate poses.

## Compliance Checklist
- [ ] The benchmark data are generated with seed 11.
- [ ] Query/database split and thresholds are unchanged.
- [ ] Ground-truth poses are not used by the method.
- [ ] Scores are produced by `scripts/evaluate.sh`.
